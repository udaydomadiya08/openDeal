Deterministic Screening Pipeline

Target discovery executes in two transparent, auditable stages:

  1. Eligibility Filtering: Binary inclusion/exclusion gates (jurisdiction, active status, mandatory financial thresholds). Excluded candidates receive explicit reasons.
  2. Multi-Factor Scoring: Eligible candidates are scored from 0 to 100 based on strategic alignment, financial scale, and penalization for missing unverified metrics.
