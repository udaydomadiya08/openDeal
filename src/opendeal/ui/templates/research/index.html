Epistemic Knowledge Architecture

OpenDeal enforces strict classification of all research assertions to prevent hallucination and false confidence:

TIER 1 • REPORTED Verbatim facts directly extracted from regulatory filings, audit reports, or official sources.
TIER 2 • DERIVED Deterministically calculated from Tier 1 facts using verifiable mathematical formulas.
TIER 3 • ANALYST ASSUMPTION Explicit forward-looking parameters supplied by investment analysts (e.g. WACC, exit multiples).
TIER 4 • AI INTERPRETATION Synthesized narrative observations grounded in verified evidence items.
TIER 5 • CONFLICT Contradictory values reported across distinct sources or periods requiring analyst reconciliation.
